# Living Archive

## Definition

The Living Archive is the historical record of observations, records, content identifiers, instruments, and provenance relationships.

## Principle

The archive preserves continuity without requiring every underlying object to be duplicated inside the repository itself.

## Archive path

The archive is a map of relationships:

- observation
- record
- CID
- instrument
- provenance
- memory
- archive

## Operational goal

The archive must support traceability, attribution where applicable, and textual continuity while preserving openness to revision and future disagreement.
