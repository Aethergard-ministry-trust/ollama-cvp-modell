# CVP-301

## Definition

CVP-301 defines the relationship between content, context, and provenance in the `commons-vector-v1` namespace.

## Normative goal

The protocol must preserve:

- content identity
- context of origin
- sequence of observations and records
- chain of custody or provenance
- dictionary-defined terminology

## Structural rules

1. Every record should reference a dictionary version.
2. Every content object should carry a CID.
3. Every instrument should identify its preceding instrument when available.
4. Local context should be recorded as a gateway/folder relationship.
5. Historical contradictions must remain visible.

## Non-goals

CVP does not establish legal jurisdiction, ownership title, banking authority, or government legitimacy. These are external systems.
