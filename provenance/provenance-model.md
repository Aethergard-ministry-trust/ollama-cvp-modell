# Provenance Model

## Overview

The provenance model defines how a record acquires historical continuity.

## Chain structure

`Observation -> Record -> CID -> Instrument -> Provenance -> Archive`

## Requirements

A provenance line should include:

- source identity or origin
- content identifier
- record timestamp or sequence
- predecessor reference
- dictionary version
- contextual coordinates

## Notes

The provenance model is a historical and evidentiary structure. It does not assign legal ownership or sovereign authority on its own.
