# Historical Sources

## Purpose

This document marks the known literature, theory, and conceptual influences relevant to the project without pretending that the work is a final or authoritative account of those traditions.

## Notes

- Hermetic and alchemical symbolism are considered part of the artistic and conceptual framing.
- IPFS and content-addressing principles provide the technical lineage for content identity.
- Provenance and archival methods provide the evidentiary structure.
- Public research should remain open to critique and correction.

## Principle

Historical and conceptual references should be traceable, contextualized, and clearly distinguished from protocol facts.
