# Methodology

## Scope

This methodology outlines the approach for structuring protocols, vocabulary, and interpretation in the `commons-vector-v1` namespace.

## Method

1. Define meaningful terms in a versioned dictionary.
2. Record local context separately from content identity.
3. Preserve provenance links between records.
4. Keep source disagreements visible.
5. Treat the archive as a living historical record rather than a closed final statement.

## Evaluation

The methodology is designed to support technical review, historical traceability, and philosophical inquiry without collapsing them into one another.
